- BM25 (Best Match 25) is a probabilistic ranking function that scores documents by their relevance to a query [^1]
	- It is the <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">strongest classical baseline</mark> in [[202605011609 - information retrieval|information retrieval]] — still widely used in production search engines despite being from the 1990s
	- A refinement of [[202605011625 - tf-idf|TF-IDF]] that fixes its two main weaknesses: linear term frequency and crude length normalization
- Origin: developed for the Okapi search system at City University, London, building on the Probabilistic Relevance Framework

> [!MATH] BM25
>
> $$\text{score}(q, d) = \sum_{t \in q} \text{idf}(t) \cdot \frac{\text{tf}(t, d) \cdot (k_1 + 1)}{\text{tf}(t, d) + k_1 \cdot \left(1 - b + b \cdot \frac{\lvert d \rvert}{\overline{\lvert d \rvert}}\right)}$$
>
> where $\lvert d \rvert$ is document length, $\overline{\lvert d \rvert}$ is the mean document length, and $k_1, b$ are free parameters

- Three improvements over [[202605011625 - tf-idf|TF-IDF]]
	1. <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">Term-frequency saturation</mark>
		- Score grows sub-linearly in $\text{tf}$ and asymptotes
		- The 100th occurrence of a term contributes far less than the 2nd
		- Controlled by $k_1$ — typical value $k_1 \in [1.2, 2.0]$
	2. <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">Document length normalization</mark>
		- Long documents are penalised so they don't dominate just by being long
		- Controlled by $b \in [0, 1]$ — typical value $b = 0.75$
		- $b = 0$ means no length normalization; $b = 1$ means full normalization
	3. Smoothed IDF
		- Uses $\log\!\left(\frac{N - \text{df}(t) + 0.5}{\text{df}(t) + 0.5}\right)$ — a probabilistic form, can go slightly negative for very common terms
- Parameter intuition
	- $k_1$ — how quickly tf saturates. Lower = saturates faster.
	- $b$ — how much to penalize long documents
	- Defaults $k_1 = 1.2, b = 0.75$ are robust across most corpora; <mark style="background: #FFB86CA6;">tune per dataset for non-trivial wins</mark>
- Variants
	- BM25F — for documents with multiple fields (title, body, anchor text), with per-field weights
	- BM25+ — adds a lower-bound on the term-frequency component to avoid over-penalizing very long docs
	- BM25L — variant with different length-normalization
- Modern context
	- Default scoring function in Lucene, Elasticsearch, OpenSearch, Tantivy
	- The <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">sparse half of hybrid retrieval</mark> — combine BM25 with a dense retriever for stronger ranking than either alone
	- Used as a hard-to-beat baseline for any new neural retrieval method
	- Surprisingly competitive against dense retrievers on out-of-domain corpora — TODO: cite — BEIR benchmark (Thakur et al. 2021)
